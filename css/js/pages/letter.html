A letter for you

Dear Gabriella,

Happy 12th birthday!

I made this little kingdom for you with my own hands, line by line, because you deserve something made just for you.

You are growing up so fast, and I am proud of the person you are becoming: kind, clever and full of light.

I want you to know a few things. You are loved. You are enough exactly as you are. And whatever you dream of becoming, I will always be cheering for you.

Thank you for being my sister. Being your brother is one of the greatest gifts I have ever received.

Now go and enjoy your day. You have earned it.

With all my love,
your brother, Jordan
